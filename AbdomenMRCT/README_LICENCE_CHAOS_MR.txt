Description: MRI T2-SPIR (40 data sets), each of which is being routinely performed to scan abdomen using different radiofrequency pulse and gradient combinations. This database also does not include any tumors or lesions at the borders of the annotated organs of interest (i.e. liver, kidneys, spleen). The data sets are acquired by a 1.5T Philips MRI, which produces 12 bit DICOM images having a resolution of 256 x 256. The ISDs vary between 5.5-9 mm (average 7.84 mm), x-y spacing is between 1.36 - 1.89 mm (average 1.61 mm) and the number of slices is between 26 and 50 (average 36). In total, 1594 slices (532 slice per sequence) will be provided for training and 1537 slices will be used for the tests.
Manual segmentations as provided by CHAOS organisers for 20 scans (1,2,3,5,8,10,13,15,19,20,21,22,31,32,33,34,36,37,38,39), 
automatic segmentations for remaining 20 scans
